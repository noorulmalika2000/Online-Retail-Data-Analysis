## Online Retail Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Online Retail dataset using Python.
The objective is to clean the data, perform exploratory data analysis,
statistical analysis, data visualization, and identify business insights.

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Tasks Performed
1. Data Import and Setup
2. Data Cleaning
3. Feature Engineering
4. Data Exploration
5. Data Wrangling
6. Statistical Analysis
7. Data Visualization
8. Business Insights

## Key Findings
- United Kingdom generated the highest sales.
- November was the highest sales month.
- 12 PM was the peak sales hour.
- PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE was the top product by revenue.
- Customer ID 14646 was the highest-value customer.

## Business Insights and Recommendations

### 1. Country Performance
The United Kingdom generated the highest revenue of approximately £7.28 million.
This indicates that the UK is the company's strongest market.
The business should focus on customer retention and marketing strategies
in this market while exploring opportunities to expand in other countries.

### 2. Seasonal Sales Trend
November recorded the highest monthly sales of approximately £1.16 million.
This suggests increased customer demand during the seasonal shopping period.
The business should prepare sufficient inventory and promotional campaigns
before November to maximize revenue.

### 3. Peak Sales Hour
12 PM was identified as the peak sales hour, generating approximately £1.37 million.
The business should ensure sufficient staffing and operational support
during peak shopping hours to improve customer experience and sales efficiency.

### 4. Customer Purchasing Behavior
The average quantity purchased per transaction was approximately 13.12 items,
with an average transaction value of £22.63.
This indicates opportunities to encourage bulk purchases and increase
average order value through product bundling and promotional offers.

### 5. High-Value Customers
Customer ID 14646 was the highest-value customer, contributing approximately £280,206.
The business should consider loyalty programs and personalized offers
to retain high-value customers.

### 6. Best-Selling Product
PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE was the top product,
generating approximately £168,470 in revenue.
The business should maintain sufficient stock of popular products
and use cross-selling strategies to increase sales.

### Conclusion
The analysis highlights the United Kingdom as the strongest market,
November as the highest-sales month, and noon as the peak sales hour.
Customer purchasing patterns and top-performing products provide
opportunities for improved inventory planning, targeted marketing,
customer retention, and increased revenue.
